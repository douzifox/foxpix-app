狐理狐图

把隐私从相册里拿出来，收好。整理截图、找出身份证银行卡、存进刷脸才能看的保险箱。全在手机上完成。

它会上传我的截图吗？

不会。识字、分类、保险箱都在手机上完成，App 没有服务器。开飞行模式也能用。详见隐私政策。

为什么要「完整相册权限」？

只有完整权限才能找到所有截图、把丢弃的照片删掉。删除永远要你先点「应用」再确认系统弹窗，删掉的进「最近删除」，30 天内能找回。

保险箱的东西存在哪？

iOS 系统钥匙串。默认随你的 Apple 账户在你的设备间同步（端到端加密），设置里能关。

自动填充怎么开？

系统设置 → 密码 → 密码选项（或「自动填充密码和通行密钥」）→ 勾上「狐理狐图」。之后登录框上方会出现狐豆豆的建议，刷脸就填。

免费能用多少？

扫描、分类、滑动整理、放篮子都免费；篮子「应用」累计 500 张、保险箱 3 条。「领养狐豆豆」一次买断，没有订阅，没有账号。换手机点「恢复购买」。

认错了怎么办？

截图详情页里能改分类；全相册里认错的照片点「不算隐私」，狐豆豆会记住。欢迎把认错的截图发到下面的邮箱，我们按它改规则。

FoxPix

Take your private stuff out of the camera roll and lock it up. Sort screenshots, find IDs and cards, keep them in a Face ID vault. All on your phone.

Does it upload my screenshots?

No. Recognition, sorting and the vault all run on-device; the app has no server. It works in Airplane Mode. See the privacy policy.

Why full photo access?

Only full access can find every screenshot and delete the ones you discard. Deletion always happens after you tap Apply and confirm the system dialog; deleted photos stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days.

Where are vault items stored?

In the iOS Keychain. They sync between your own devices through iCloud Keychain (end-to-end encrypted) by default; you can turn that off in Settings.

How do I enable AutoFill?

iOS Settings → Passwords → Password Options (or AutoFill Passwords and Passkeys) → turn on FoxPix. Suggestions then appear above login fields; Face ID fills them.

What's free?

Scanning, sorting, swiping and the basket are free; applying the basket is limited to 500 photos in total and the vault to 3 items. The full version is a one-time purchase: no subscription, no account. Tap Restore Purchases on a new phone.
