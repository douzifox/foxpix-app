iPhone 截图太多，怎么整理？

打开狐理狐图点「扫描截图」，狐豆豆会在手机上读出每张截图的文字，分成账号与财务信息、聊天、购物快递、二维码、网页等类别。然后一张张滑：留着的收进对应相簿，不要的放进篮子，最后点「应用」一次删掉。删之前系统会再确认一次，删掉的在「最近删除」里留 30 天。

怎么找出相册里的身份证、银行卡照片？

切到顶上的「全相册」，狐豆豆会翻一遍所有照片，把拍过的证件、银行卡、带卡号或账号的图挑出来，也会找出重复的照片。要留的号码可以直接存进保险箱，原图再删掉。

跟 iPhone 自带的「照片」有什么不一样？

「照片」能搜到图里的字，但不会按用途把截图分好、帮你挑出该删的，也没有一个要解锁才能看的地方放证件和密码。狐理狐图专门做这几件事，而且全在手机上完成。

它会上传我的截图吗？

不会。识字、分类、保险箱都在手机上完成，App 没有服务器。开飞行模式也能用。详见隐私政策。

为什么要「完整相册权限」？

只有完整权限才能找到所有截图、把丢弃的照片删掉。删除永远要你先点「应用」再确认系统弹窗，删掉的进「最近删除」，30 天内能找回。

保险箱的东西存在哪？

iOS 系统钥匙串。默认随你的 Apple 账户在你的设备间同步（端到端加密），设置里能关。

自动填充怎么开？

系统设置 → 密码 → 密码选项（或「自动填充密码和通行密钥」）→ 勾上「狐理狐图」。之后登录框上方会出现狐豆豆的建议，解锁就填。

免费能用多少？

扫描、分类、滑动整理、放篮子都免费；篮子「应用」累计 500 张、保险箱 3 条。「领养狐豆豆」一次买断，没有订阅，没有账号。换手机点「恢复购买」。

能导入哪些密码文件？

保险箱 → + → 「从文件导入」，选别的密码 App 导出的 CSV：苹果「密码」、1Password、Bitwarden、Chrome、Edge、Firefox 都认，别的只要有标题、网址、账号、密码几列也能读。导完记得把文件删掉，导入页上有按钮。

认错了怎么办？

截图详情页里能改分类；全相册里认错的照片点「不算隐私」，狐豆豆会记住。欢迎把认错的截图发到下面的邮箱，我们按它改规则。

FoxPix

Take your private stuff out of the camera roll and lock it up. Sort screenshots, find IDs and cards, keep them in a Face ID vault. All on your phone.

FoxPix (狐理狐图) is an iPhone app (iOS 17 or later). It reads the text in your screenshots on the phone, sorts them into categories and helps you pick the ones to delete. It can also go through your whole photo library to find photos of IDs and bank cards, plus duplicates. Logins, passwords and card numbers go into a vault you unlock with Face ID, with AutoFill on login fields. No sign-up, no server, nothing uploaded. Free to download; the full version is a one-time purchase.

Too many screenshots on my iPhone. How do I clean them up?

Open FoxPix and tap Scan screenshots. It reads the text of each one on the phone and sorts them into categories like personal info and logins, chats, shopping and delivery, QR codes and web pages. Then swipe through: keep the ones you want in matching albums, drop the rest in the basket, and tap Apply to delete them at once. iOS asks you to confirm, and deleted photos stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days.

How do I find photos of my ID or bank cards in the camera roll?

Switch to Whole Library at the top. FoxPix goes through every photo and picks out IDs, bank cards and images with card or account numbers, and finds duplicates too. Save the numbers you need to the vault, then delete the originals.

How is it different from the built-in Photos app?

Photos can search text in images, but it doesn't sort screenshots by what they're for, pick out the ones to delete, or give you a locked place for IDs and passwords. FoxPix does exactly that, all on the phone.

Does it upload my screenshots?

No. Recognition, sorting and the vault all run on-device; the app has no server. It works in Airplane Mode. See the privacy policy.

Why full photo access?

Only full access can find every screenshot and delete the ones you discard. Deletion always happens after you tap Apply and confirm the system dialog; deleted photos stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days.

Where are vault items stored?

In the iOS Keychain. They sync between your own devices through iCloud Keychain (end-to-end encrypted) by default; you can turn that off in Settings.

How do I enable AutoFill?

iOS Settings → Passwords → Password Options (or AutoFill Passwords and Passkeys) → turn on FoxPix. Suggestions then appear above login fields; Face ID fills them.

What's free?

Scanning, sorting, swiping and the basket are free; applying the basket is limited to 500 photos in total and the vault to 3 items. The full version is a one-time purchase: no subscription, no account. Tap Restore Purchases on a new phone.

Which password files can I import?

Vault → + → Import from a file, then pick the CSV exported by your other password app: Apple Passwords, 1Password, Bitwarden, Chrome, Edge and Firefox are recognized; any file with title, URL, username and password columns works too. Delete the file afterwards; the import page has a button for it.
