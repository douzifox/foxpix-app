相册里的身份证、银行卡照片安全吗？该怎么处理？

照片不会自己泄露，但它们比你想的暴露得多：会随 iCloud 照片同步，会被网盘自动备份，借手机时一滑就能看到，在相册里搜几个字就能全部找出来。比较稳妥的做法是：把需要的信息存进加密、要解锁才能看的地方，然后删掉原图。

先看看你的相册里有多少

iPhone 的「照片」能搜到图片里的文字。打开「照片」→ 搜索，分别搜「身份证」「卡号」「银行」「密码」「验证码」。我们自己第一次完整扫描时，3089 张截图里有 238 张带着证件、卡号或账号密码。

几种处理办法

办法挡得住谁还会同步、上传吗适合
放进「已隐藏」相簿（iOS 16 起要面容 ID 才能打开）借手机的人会，照常随 iCloud 照片同步少量、偶尔看
备忘录里扫描成笔记，再给笔记加锁借手机的人；锁定的笔记内容是加密的同步，但内容加密少量证件
密码管理器（苹果「密码」、1Password、Bitwarden 等）借手机的人；加密存储加密同步账号密码、银行卡，要手动录入
信息存好以后删掉原图所有人删了就不会再传所有情况，最彻底

只是藏起来，照片还在，还会同步。真正降低风险的是「信息存进加密的地方 + 删掉原图」。删完记得去「最近删除」再删一次，不然 30 天后才真的删掉。

狐理狐图能把全相册翻一遍，挑出拍过的身份证、银行卡和带账号的照片；认出来的信息一键存进保险箱，原图就能放心删。

Is it safe to keep photos of my ID or bank card in my camera roll?

The photos won't leak on their own, but they're more exposed than you'd think: they sync with iCloud Photos, get backed up by cloud drive apps, show up when someone borrows your phone and swipes, and turn up in seconds with a text search in Photos. The safer approach: save the information somewhere encrypted that needs unlocking, then delete the original photo.

First, see how many you have

The Photos app on iPhone can search text inside images. Open Photos → Search and try "card", "bank", "password", "passport", "verification code". The first time we scanned our own screenshots, 238 out of 3,089 held IDs, card numbers or logins.

Hiding a photo doesn't remove it, and it keeps syncing. What actually lowers the risk is saving the information somewhere encrypted and deleting the original. Afterwards, empty Recently Deleted too, or the photo stays there for 30 days.

iPhone 截图太多怎么整理？

所有截图都在「照片」的「截屏」相簿里，可以从这里一批批看、一批批删。难的不是删，是几千张里分不清哪些还有用：有的存着卡号密码，有的是想留的聊天记录，更多的是看过就没用的网页和通知。先按用途分开，再决定留哪些、删哪些，会快很多。

用系统自带的办法

  1. 打开「照片」，在「媒体类型」下面找到「截屏」
  2. 按时间从最老的看起，越老的越可能没用了
  3. 要找某一张，直接搜图里的字
  4. 想留的放进自己建的相簿，比如「票据」「聊天」

狐理狐图会一张张识字，再看版面（聊天气泡、网页地址栏、App 底部标签），把截图按「该拿它怎么办」分成隐私、社交、文字、其他，每一类附一句建议。左滑丢弃、右滑收藏，收藏的按分类放进相簿。删不删永远你说了算。

How do I organize thousands of screenshots on iPhone?

All your screenshots are in the Screenshots album in Photos, where you can review and delete them in batches. The hard part isn't deleting, it's telling which ones still matter: some hold card numbers and passwords, some are chats you want to keep, and most are web pages and notifications you'll never look at again. Sorting them by what they're for first makes the rest much faster.

With the built-in Photos app

  1. Open Photos and find Screenshots under Media Types
  2. Start from the oldest; older ones are more likely to be useless
  3. To find a specific one, search for text in it
  4. Move the keepers into your own albums, such as "Receipts" or "Chats"

FoxPix reads the text in every screenshot and looks at the layout (chat bubbles, address bars, app tab bars), then groups them by what to do with them: Private, Social, Text and Other, each with a suggestion. Swipe left to discard, right to keep; kept ones go into albums by category. Whether to delete is always your call.

有没有不上传、全在手机上处理的截图整理 App？

有。iPhone 本身就能在手机上识别图片里的文字，App 不需要把截图传到服务器也能读懂它。判断一个 App 有没有上传，可以看三点：开飞行模式还能不能正常用；App Store 页面上「App 隐私」那一栏写了收集什么；要不要注册账号。

狐理狐图的识字、分类、保险箱都在 iPhone 本机完成，用的是系统自带的文字识别。App 没有服务器，不用注册，开飞行模式也能用。

Is there a screenshot organizer that works fully on-device?

Yes. iPhone can recognize text in images on the device itself, so an app doesn't need to send your screenshots to a server to understand them. To check whether an app uploads anything, look at three things: does it still work in Airplane Mode; what does the App Privacy section on its App Store page say it collects; does it make you sign up.

In FoxPix, text recognition, sorting and the vault all run on your iPhone, using the system's built-in text recognition. The app has no server, needs no account, and works in Airplane Mode.

截图里的账号密码怎么存才安全？

存进密码管理器，然后删掉截图。截图就是一张普通照片，跟着相册同步、备份，谁拿到手机都能看；密码管理器是加密的，要解锁才能看，还能在登录时自动填充。

可以选的

  • 苹果「密码」App（iOS 18 起；更早的系统在「设置」→「密码」）：系统自带，能自动填充，同步到你的苹果设备
  • 1Password、Bitwarden 等：跨平台，安卓、Windows 上也能用
  • 备忘录加锁：也是加密的，但不能自动填充

这几个都要你自己把账号密码一条条录进去。截图多的话，这一步最费劲。

狐理狐图能从截图里认出账号、密码、卡号，一键存成一条，存在 iPhone 的钥匙串里，登录时自动填充。随时能导出：Bitwarden 格式（全部类型），或者苹果「密码」认的表格（账号密码），换别的密码管理器也带得走。

How should I store passwords I took screenshots of?

Put them in a password manager, then delete the screenshots. A screenshot is just a photo: it syncs and backs up with your library, and anyone holding your phone can see it. A password manager is encrypted, needs unlocking, and can fill in logins for you.

Your choices

  • Apple Passwords (iOS 18 and later; on earlier versions it's under Settings → Passwords): built in, does AutoFill, syncs across your Apple devices
  • 1Password, Bitwarden and others: cross-platform, also on Android and Windows
  • A locked note: also encrypted, but no AutoFill

All of these need you to type each login in by hand. With a lot of screenshots, that's the painful part.

FoxPix reads logins and card numbers from your screenshots and saves each as one item in one tap. Items live in the iOS Keychain and fill in when you sign in. Export any time as a Bitwarden file (every item type) or a CSV Apple Passwords reads (logins), so you can take everything to another password manager.

iPhone 怎么批量删除截图？

在「照片」的「截屏」相簿里点「选择」，手指按住一张缩略图横着一路滑过去，就能连着选一大片，再点删除。删之前先把里面要用的信息存好：卡号、密码、地址这类，删了 30 天后就找不回来了。

  1. 「照片」→「媒体类型」→「截屏」→ 右上角「选择」
  2. 从一张开始横着滑、再往下拖，连续选中
  3. 点垃圾桶删除
  4. 删掉的在「最近删除」里留 30 天；要立刻腾出空间，去那里再删一次

狐理狐图先把截图分好，你决定丢的都先放进篮子。最后点一次「应用」，系统再确认一次，才一起删掉。带隐私的可以先存进保险箱再删。

How do I bulk delete screenshots on iPhone?

In the Screenshots album in Photos, tap Select, then drag your finger across the thumbnails to select a whole run at once, and tap Delete. Before deleting, save anything you still need: card numbers, passwords and addresses are gone for good 30 days later.

  1. Photos → Media Types → Screenshots → Select (top right)
  2. Drag sideways from one photo, then down, to select a range
  3. Tap the trash icon
  4. Deleted photos stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days; delete them there to free space right away

FoxPix sorts your screenshots first, and everything you choose to discard waits in the basket. Tap Apply once and iOS asks you to confirm before anything is deleted. Save the private ones to the vault before they go.

iCloud 照片会把截图传到哪？安全吗？

开着 iCloud 照片，每张截图都会上传到你自己的 iCloud，放进「已隐藏」的也一样。传输和存储都是加密的，但默认情况下密钥在苹果那里；只有打开「高级数据保护」，照片才是端到端加密，连苹果也看不到。这个选项默认是关的。

想更安全

  • 打开高级数据保护：「设置」→ 你的名字 →「iCloud」→「高级数据保护」。开之前要设好恢复联系人或恢复密钥，不然忘了密码，苹果也帮不了你
  • 不需要的证件照、带密码的截图直接删掉；删掉以后，iCloud 里的也跟着删
  • 另外留意网盘 App 的「自动备份相册」，它们会再存一份原图

狐理狐图保险箱里的东西走 iCloud 钥匙串同步，钥匙串本来就是端到端加密的，不用开高级数据保护，苹果也看不到。不想同步，设置里能关，关了只留在这台手机上。

Where does iCloud Photos send my screenshots? Is it safe?

With iCloud Photos on, every screenshot uploads to your own iCloud, including the ones in the Hidden album. They're encrypted in transit and in storage, but by default Apple holds the keys. Only with Advanced Data Protection turned on are photos end-to-end encrypted, so not even Apple can read them. It's off by default.

To make it safer

  • Turn on Advanced Data Protection: Settings → your name → iCloud → Advanced Data Protection. Set up a recovery contact or recovery key first; if you lose access, Apple can't help you get back in
  • Delete ID photos and password screenshots you no longer need; deleting them also removes them from iCloud
  • Check cloud drive apps for "back up camera roll"; they keep another copy of the originals

FoxPix vault items sync through iCloud Keychain, which is end-to-end encrypted on its own, with or without Advanced Data Protection. You can turn sync off in Settings to keep items on this phone only.

给别人看手机里的照片，怎么防止被翻到别的？

用「引导式访问」，把触控关掉：对方只能看你打开的那一张，滑不走，也退不出去。

  1. 先打开：「设置」→「辅助功能」→「引导式访问」
  2. 打开要给人看的照片，连按三下侧边按钮
  3. 点左下角「选项」，关掉「触控」，再点「开始」
  4. 拿回手机后连按三下侧边按钮，用面容 ID 或密码结束

平时不想被看到的，可以放进「已隐藏」相簿，要面容 ID 才能打开。

从根上说，相册里没有就翻不到：用狐理狐图把证件、卡号、密码存进保险箱，再删掉原图。

How do I show someone a photo without them swiping through the rest?

Use Guided Access with touch turned off. They can only look at the photo you opened; they can't swipe away or leave the app.

  1. Turn it on first: Settings → Accessibility → Guided Access
  2. Open the photo you want to show and triple-click the side button
  3. Tap Options at the bottom left, turn off Touch, then tap Start
  4. When you get the phone back, triple-click the side button and end with Face ID or your passcode

For photos you never want seen, the Hidden album needs Face ID to open.
